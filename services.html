What We Handle For You

STRATEGIC BUYER SOURCING

We identify international buyers, understand their product requirements, and connect them with suitable Indian manufacturers to create reliable sourcing opportunities and long-term business growth.

BUYER REQUIREMENTS & MANUFACTURER SELECTION

We align your product requirements, quality expectations, target costs, order quantities, and delivery timelines with the right manufacturing partners across India, ensuring reliable sourcing, competitive pricing, and consistent quality.

COMPETITIVE COSTING & PRICE NEGOTIATION

We coordinate transparent costing and competitive price negotiations, balancing buyer budgets with manufacturing capabilities and expected quality standards.

DESIGN & PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

We coordinate design development, fabric and trim selection, sampling, fit approvals, and product specifications to bring buyer concepts to production.

SAMPLE DEVELOPMENT & APPROVAL

We manage sample development, size-set coordination, buyer feedback, and approvals to establish a clear benchmark for bulk production.

QUALITY ASSURANCE & PRODUCTION CONTROL

Our greatest achievement is the growth of our buyers and the brands we help build through uncompromising quality. We coordinate quality checks from pre-production through final inspection, ensuring products meet approved specifications and agreed quality standards.

ORDER MANAGEMENT & PRODUCTION FOLLOW-UP

We track order progress, materials, production schedules, and time-and-action plans while maintaining clear communication between buyers and manufacturers.

EXPORT DOCUMENTATION & SHIPMENT COORDINATION

We coordinate export documentation, packing requirements, dispatch schedules, and shipment follow-up to support smooth and timely delivery.
